Privacy policy

Pratap Jewellers Portal (“the portal”) is an internal tool for Pratap Jewellers staff in Kalyan, Maharashtra. Last updated: 12 November 2026.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, the portal receives your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We do not receive or store your Google password.

How we use it

Your email is used only to check that you are a member of staff and to show you the company systems, tasks and attendance information you are allowed to see. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers or used for marketing.

Where it is kept

Staff details are kept in the company’s own Google Workspace files. The portal keeps a sign-in session in your browser so you do not need to sign in every time. Signing out removes it.

Who can see it

Only authorised Pratap Jewellers management and the MIS team.

Contact

For any question or to ask for your data to be removed, contact the MIS team: rohantodkar@pratapjewellers.in.
